Healthcare & clinical systems

Every signal deserves to be heard.

When a patient monitor raises an urgent alert, that warning needs a reliable path to the people responsible for care. Firelight Guardian is a proposed way to help protect that path.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Conceptual design; not medically validated, clinically deployed or cleared.

DESIGN PRIORITIES
01

Persistent alarms

Explore reliable acknowledgment and escalation without silent cancellation.

02

Independent monitoring

Critical signals should remain visible across distinct software layers.

03

Patient-first verification

Human factors, clinical requirements and regulatory validation must be established before use.

WHAT WE STILL NEED TO TEST

What happens when an alarm gets lost?

This healthcare concept needs testing for missed signals, false alarms, outages, and real clinical workflows. It has not been cleared or tested for patient use.
