Home & consumer

Know what your connected home is doing.

Which devices can unlock doors, change settings, or send alerts? An inspection could help a homeowner see those connections, review the risks, and get a clear report.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

Interface prototype; not an accredited or operating inspection service.

DESIGN PRIORITIES
01

Inventory your environment

Identify connected devices, owners and control paths.

02

Check what fails

Review account permissions, alert paths and offline behavior.

03

Practical recommendations

Prioritize clear, documented remediation steps for owners and property managers.

WHAT WE STILL NEED TO TEST

Could a homeowner understand the findings?

The goal is a practical checklist and a report people can use. The software interface is a prototype, not an accredited inspection service.
