Our mission
EMMY'S PROMISE

Every life holds a future of moments.

The founding purpose behind Brad-Core is to give families more of them. A child keeps a parent. A parent watches a child grow up. A loved one comes home.

That purpose shapes our demand for accountable alarms, independent safety authority, careful engineering reviews, and transparent verification. It is the reason we build, not a substitute for evidence that the technology works.

OUR COMMITMENT

Protect people. Earn trust. Build for the long term.

Brad-Core is a for-profit engineering venture seeking to build valuable safety technology and a durable legacy—without compromising the purpose behind it.
