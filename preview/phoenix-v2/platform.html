THE ARCHITECTURAL IDEA

Separate intelligence from authority.

Brad-Core's candidate platform divides untrusted AI reasoning, deterministic validation, protected enforcement, and independent evidence handling. The interface is designed to be bounded and reviewable.

01

P-Core / Lucy

The untrusted reasoning environment develops proposals but does not independently authorize consequential actions.

02

D-Core + Sheriff

Candidate deterministic decision and enforcement subsystems check safety conditions and authority boundaries.

03

M-Core

A proposed evidence and telemetry layer makes abnormal events available for investigation and validation.

ONE DISCIPLINE, DISTINCT SAFETY CASES

Changing the environment changes the rules.

Engineering claims must survive contact with hardware.

A frozen interface is not automatic certification. Electrical shutdown is not the same as mechanical safety. Software timing cannot substitute for target-instrumented measurements. Those boundaries are part of our development approach.
