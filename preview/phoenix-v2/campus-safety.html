THE DESIGN DIRECTION

Purpose-built for its own risks.

The current concept emphasizes multiple independent signals, protected evacuation paths, and timely responder handoff. Earlier acoustic and light-based suppression features were removed from the proposed direction.

01

Local awareness

Cross-check signals while preserving human context and judgment.

02

Safe movement

Design for evacuation and emergency access as first-class safety requirements.

03

Responder context

Make reliable incident and occupancy information available for authorized response.
